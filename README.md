# ESO

A social platform for introverts and reflective minds. Dark aesthetic. Minimal noise.

## Why ESO

Eso means "inner" or "within". Most social platforms reward attention-seeking. Eso rewards depth. No follower counts. No likes. No performance metrics. Just thoughts, reflection, and conversation.

## Circles

Circles are spaces where people gather around shared interests or emotions. Unlike traditional groups, circles are fluid. A single post can exist in multiple circles simultaneously. You join circles that resonate. You leave when they don't.

Each circle has a topic limit to keep conversations focused. When a circle reaches its limit, create a new one or find another space.

## Rooms

Three rooms define the experience:

* Dark Room - Release emotions without judgment. Confessions, regrets, shadows.
* Fantasy Room - Imagine possibilities. Share dreams. Explore what could be.
* Philo Room - Question everything. Reflect deeply. Explore meaning.

## How to Use

Create an account. Choose a username. Pick a room. Join or create a circle. Post your thoughts. React to others. Reply when moved to. That's it.

No algorithms. No feeds optimized for engagement. Just chronological posts in the circles you choose.

## Tech Stack

* React + Vite
* Node.js + Express
