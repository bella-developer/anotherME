===========================================
RENDER ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
Copy these to: https://dashboard.render.com/
Service: anotherme-backend → Environment
===========================================

Add these 4 variables:

1. GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID
   Value: [Your Client ID from Google Console]

2. GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET
   Value: [Your Client Secret from Google Console]

3. GOOGLE_CALLBACK_URL
   Value: https://anotherme-backend.onrender.com/api/auth/google/callback

4. FRONTEND_URL
   Value: https://anothermee.vercel.app

===========================================
After adding, click "Save Changes"
Render will automatically redeploy (~2 mins)
===========================================

GOOGLE CONSOLE REDIRECT URI:
Make sure this is in your Google OAuth Client:
https://anotherme-backend.onrender.com/api/auth/google/callback
